# Code of conduct

Be decent to the people you're working with. Disagree about code as hard as you like; don't make it about the person.

Not acceptable in issues, pull requests or discussions: harassment, slurs, personal attacks, posting other people's private information, and sexual or violent content. Uploading pictures of real people without their consent to this project's issues or tests also falls here.

I may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues, and block people who ignore this. To report a problem, use the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mention it's about conduct.
